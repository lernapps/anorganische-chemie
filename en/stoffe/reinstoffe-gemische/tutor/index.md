## Context: the page “Pure substances and mixtures”

Pure substances consist of a single kind of substance, mixtures of several. This is how you tell them apart.

### Why

Almost everything around you is a substance: water, air, metal, milk. To sort substances, first ask: is this a single kind of substance, or are several mixed together?

### Rule

- **Pure substance**: A pure substance consists of only one kind of substance, for example pure water or pure oxygen.

- **Mixture**: A mixture consists of several substances, for example air, milk or granite.

A pure substance always has the same properties, for example a fixed boiling point. The substances in a mixture can often be separated again without a chemical reaction, for example by filtering or evaporating.

Pure substances and mixtures compared:
- Feature | Pure substance | Mixture
- Ingredients | one kind of substance | several substances
- Examples | pure water, oxygen, table salt | air, milk, granite

### Example

**Example: Is air a pure substance?**

Air consists mainly of nitrogen and oxygen, plus other gases. So several substances are mixed. Air is therefore a **mixture**, not a pure substance.

### Exercise gemische-finden

- Task: Which of these substances are mixtures?
- Answer format: choose all right answers
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ air – Air consists of nitrogen, oxygen and other gases. Several substances are mixed.
  - ✓ milk – Milk contains water, fats and proteins, among other things. It is a mixture.
  - ✗ pure water – Pure (distilled) water consists only of water. It is a pure substance.
  - ✗ pure oxygen – Pure oxygen consists of only one kind of substance. It is a pure substance.
- Solution: air; milk
- Explanation: Mixtures consist of several substances. Air and milk are mixtures; pure water and pure oxygen are pure substances.

### Exercise salzwasser-reinstoff

- Task: Salt water is a pure substance.
- Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
- Solution: The statement is false.
- Explanation: Salt water consists of water and salt. Two substances are mixed, so it is a mixture. If the water evaporates, the salt stays behind.
