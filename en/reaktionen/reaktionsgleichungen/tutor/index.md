## Context: the page “Reaction equations”

A reaction equation shows which substances react. There must be the same number of atoms on both sides.

### Why

With a reaction equation you describe a reaction briefly and precisely: which substances go in, which come out and in which ratio.

### Rule

- **Reaction equation**: A reaction equation describes a reaction with formulas. The starting substances are on the left of the arrow, the products on the right.

- **Coefficient**: A coefficient is the big number in front of a formula. It tells how often the formula occurs.

The atoms are kept in a reaction. So every kind of atom must occur equally often on the left and on the right. You achieve that with coefficients. You never change the indices in the formulas, otherwise it would be a different substance.

$\ce{2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}$

### Example

**Example: Writing the equation for water**

1. Write the formulas: $\ce{H2 + O2 -> H2O}$. On the left there are 2 oxygen atoms, on the right only 1.
2. Put the 2 in front of $\ce{H2O}$: $\ce{H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}$. Now there are 4 hydrogen atoms on the right, only 2 on the left.
3. Put the 2 in front of $\ce{H2}$: $\ce{2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O}$. On the left and on the right there are 4 H and 2 O.

### Exercise koeffizient-wasser

- Task: Which number belongs in place of x? x H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
- Answer format: type a number
- Solution: 2
- Explanation: On the right there are 2 · 2 = 4 hydrogen atoms. So you need 2 particles of H₂ on the left.
- Typical mistakes and what the app says:
  - 1: Then there would be only 2 hydrogen atoms on the left, but 4 (2 · 2) on the right.

### Exercise gleichung-schritte

- Task: Put the steps for writing a reaction equation in the right order.
- Answer format: put the items in the right order (the app shows them shuffled)
- Solution: 1. Write down the formulas of the starting substances and products, 2. Count the atoms on the left and on the right, 3. Put coefficients in front of the formulas, 4. Count again and check
- Explanation: First come the formulas, then you count, then you balance with coefficients, and finally you check.
