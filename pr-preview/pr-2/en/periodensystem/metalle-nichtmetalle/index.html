Metals and non-metals

On this page

  1. Why
  2. Rule
  3. Example
  4. Practice

Why

A metal spoon, a plastic bag, a piece of sulfur: substances behave very differently. A lot of it depends on whether an element is a metal.

Rule

Metal

Metals conduct electricity and heat well, can be formed into sheets and wires and have a metallic lustre.

Non-metal

Non-metals do not have these metal properties. Examples are hydrogen, carbon, nitrogen, oxygen and sulfur.

Metalloid

Metalloids stand in the periodic table between metals and non-metals and cannot clearly be put into either group. Examples are boron and silicon.

In the periodic table the metals are on the left and below a line from boron to astatine. The non-metals are on the right above this line.

Example

Is aluminium a metal?

Aluminium is in group 13, period 3, on the left and below the line from boron to astatine. It conducts electricity and can be rolled into foil. Aluminium is a metal.

Which property do metals have?

  • They conduct electric current well. right answer

  • They do not conduct electric current.

    That applies more to non-metals. Metals conduct current well.

  • They are always gaseous at room temperature.

    Metals are solid at room temperature; only mercury is liquid. No metal is gaseous.
