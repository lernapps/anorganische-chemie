### metalle-fest (true-false)

- Link: [./#metalle-fest](./#metalle-fest), in the section „Practice“
- Task: All metals are solid at room temperature.
- Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
- Solution: The statement is false.
- Explanation: Mercury is a metal and liquid at room temperature. It melts at about −39 °C.

### metall-eigenschaft (single-choice)

- Link: [./#metall-eigenschaft](./#metall-eigenschaft), in the section „Practice“
- Task: Which property do metals have?
- Answer format: choose one answer
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ They conduct electric current well. – A high electrical conductivity is one of the typical properties of metals.
  - ✗ They do not conduct electric current. – That applies more to non-metals. Metals conduct current well.
  - ✗ They are always gaseous at room temperature. – Metals are solid at room temperature; only mercury is liquid. No metal is gaseous.
- Solution: They conduct electric current well.
- Explanation: Metals conduct electricity and heat well, can be formed and have a lustre.

### bor-halbmetall (true-false)

- Link: [./#bor-halbmetall](./#bor-halbmetall), in the section „Practice“
- Task: Boron and silicon are metalloids.
- Answer format: choose “true” or “false”
- Solution: The statement is true.
- Explanation: Boron and silicon stand at the border between metals and non-metals and count as metalloids.
