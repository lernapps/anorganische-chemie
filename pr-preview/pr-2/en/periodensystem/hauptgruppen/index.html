The eight main groups

On this page

  1. Why
  2. Rule
  3. Example
  4. Practice

Why

Elements in one group behave in a similar way. If you know the main group, you can predict how an element behaves.

Rule

Main group

The eight main groups are groups 1, 2 and 13 to 18 of the periodic table. They are numbered I to VIII.

The eight main groups with their elements (without the superheavy ones)
Main groupGroupNameElements
I1alkali metals (and hydrogen)H, Li, Na, K, Rb, Cs, Fr
II2alkaline earth metalsBe, Mg, Ca, Sr, Ba, Ra
III13boron groupB, Al, Ga, In, Tl
IV14carbon–silicon groupC, Si, Ge, Sn, Pb
V15nitrogen–phosphorus groupN, P, As, Sb, Bi
VI16chalcogensO, S, Se, Te, Po
VII17halogensF, Cl, Br, I, At
VIII18noble gasesHe, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn

For the main groups the number equals the number of electrons in the outermost shell, the outer electrons. Helium is the exception: it has only 2 outer electrons, because its first shell cannot hold more.

The elements of the alkali metals, alkaline earth metals, halogens and noble gases are especially similar. The noble gases are odourless, colourless and gaseous.

Example

Chlorine in main group VII

Chlorine is in group 17, so in main group VII (17 − 10 = 7). Its electrons sit on shells 2, 8 and 7. The outermost shell has 7 electrons, exactly the number of the main group. Chlorine is a halogen.

Exercise

Match each group of the periodic table with its name.

Choose the match for each entry.

Right answer: Group 1 – alkali metals (and hydrogen); Group 2 – alkaline earth metals; Group 16 – chalcogens; Group 17 – halogens; Group 18 – noble gases

Groups 1 and 2 are the alkali and alkaline earth metals, group 16 the chalcogens, group 17 the halogens and group 18 the noble gases.

  1. Task no. 1

    How many outer electrons does an atom of Caesium (symbol Cs) have?

    Hint

    For the main groups the number of outer electrons is the number of the main group (I to VIII). Helium is the exception.

    Solution
    1. Caesium has 1 outer electrons.

    Right answer: 1

    Right answer: 8

Exercise

Three worked tasks, each with its solution:

  1. Task no. 1

    Radon is in group 18 of the periodic table. In which main group (I to VIII, as a number 1 to 8) is it?

    Hint

    For groups 13 to 18 you subtract 10.

    Solution
    1. 18 − 10 = 8

    Right answer: 8

  2. Task no. 2

    Boron is in group 13 of the periodic table. In which main group (I to VIII, as a number 1 to 8) is it?

    Hint

    For groups 13 to 18 you subtract 10.

    Solution

    Right answer: 3

  3. Task no. 3

    Bismuth is in group 15 of the periodic table. In which main group (I to VIII, as a number 1 to 8) is it?

    Hint

    For groups 13 to 18 you subtract 10.

    Solution

    Right answer: 5
